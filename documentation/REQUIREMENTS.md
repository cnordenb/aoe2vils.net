## The page

1. The page's title and its heading will read "aoe2vils.net". [b]
2. The page will open on the Villagers Required tab on every visit. [b]
3. Every setting and row will be as it was after the page is reloaded, except the tab. [b]
4. The page will fit the window with no page scrolling; only dropdown lists will scroll. [a:1] [b]
5. The page will show no ads and set no cookies. [r]
6. Opened as a local file, the page will work with no network connection. [b]
7. The page will load no file from any address other than its own. [b]
8. The page will use dark colours when the browser asks for a dark colour scheme. [b]
9. The page will have no undo. [a:18]
10. No message will appear to confirm an action, such as "Page cleared" or "Unit added". [a:19] [b]
11. The page will show no help text, such as "Search for a unit" or a description of Conscription. [a:15]
12. Ticking or unticking a checkbox will not move any other part of the page, except Buildings, which shows and hides
    the # Builders column. [b]
13. A hidden button will keep its space, so that showing it moves nothing. [a:20] [b]
14. Costs, times, civs, civ bonuses, team bonuses, tech trees, ages and lines will match aoe2techtree.net. [b]
15. Gather rates, and times changed by a bonus or technology, will be shown to one decimal. [b]

## Tabs and the header

16. The page will have two tabs, Villagers Required and Transition Eco, in that order. [b]
17. Your Civilization will be a dropdown that can be searched by typing, with Generic by default. [a:2]
18. Generic will have no civ bonuses. [a:2]
19. Allied Civilization(s) will be a dropdown, left of Your Civilization, that takes 0 to 7 allies, 0 by default.
    [a:13]
20. The label of Allied Civilization(s) will show how many allies are picked, out of 7, from "0/7" up. [b]
21. Each ally's team bonus will apply to the numbers like any other bonus. [a:5] [a:13]
22. The same team bonus will never count twice. [b]
23. A team bonus in the bonus line will end with its ally in brackets, e.g. "Stables work 20% faster (from Huns
    Ally)". [a:13]
24. With no civ bonus that changes the numbers, the bonus line will read "No applicable civ bonuses.". [a:15]
25. With allies but no team bonus of theirs that changes the numbers, the bonus line will read "No applicable team
    bonuses from allies.". [a:15]
26. A civ bonus's label will say what the bonus does to a number, with one verb and no explanation after a comma,
    e.g. "Foragers work +15% faster". [b]
27. A bonus that changes only buildings will be left out of the bonus line while Buildings is unticked, unless it
    changes a row in the table. [b]
28. A bonus that changes only naval entries will be left out of the bonus line while Naval is unticked, unless it
    changes a row in the table. [b]
29. The Age radios will be Dark Age, Feudal Age, Castle Age and Imperial Age, with Imperial Age by default. [a:6]
30. A Berber ally will make Genitours available, an Italian ally the Condottiero, and a Vietnamese ally the Imperial
    Skirmisher. [b]

    Regional & Unique is ticked. [b]

    "(Elite) Plumed Archer". [a:9] [a:21]
54. A longer line will be named after its first unit, with "-line", e.g. "Militia-line" and "Battering Ram-line".
    [a:9] [b]
55. Picking an entry that is already in the table will add 1 to its # Buildings. [b]
56. The dropdown will stay open while Naval, Buildings, Full Tech Tree, Include Regional & Unique, the Age radios or
    Post-Imp are changed. [a:16] [a:29] [b]
57. Any dropdown will open when clicked while another dropdown is open. [a:30]
58. The arrow of a searchable dropdown will open it while it is closed and close it while it is open. [b]
59. The arrow will point down while its dropdown is closed and up while it is open. [b]
60. Naval, Buildings and Full Tech Tree (or Include Regional & Unique) will be below the dropdown's label. [a:39]
61. Naval will be unticked by default. [a:7]
62. While Naval is unticked, the dropdown will list no Dock, no unit of the Dock, no Fish Trap and no Harbor. [a:7] [b]
63. Buildings will be unticked by default. [a:36]
64. While Buildings is unticked, the dropdown will list no building. [a:36]
65. Full Tech Tree will be unticked by default. [a:11]
66. Full Tech Tree will be hidden while Your Civilization is Generic, and Include Regional & Unique will be in its
    place. [a:24] [b]

    unique units. [a:11] [b]
68. With Full Tech Tree ticked, regional units will be listed for every civ. [b]
69. With Full Tech Tree ticked, civ bonuses, team bonuses and unique technologies will not apply. [a:23]

    are inhibited when Full Tech Tree is enabled.". [a:41]
71. With Full Tech Tree ticked, every technology checkbox will be available to the civ. [a:11]
72. Include Regional & Unique will be unticked by default. [b]
73. The dropdown will offer "Custom Unit..." first under Units, and "Custom Building..." first under Buildings while
    Buildings is ticked. [b]
74. The table will hold at most 5 custom units and 5 custom buildings. [b]
75. A custom unit will have a wood, food and gold cost of 0 to 999 each and a training time from 1 to 999 seconds. [b]
76. A custom building will have a wood, gold and stone cost of 0 to 9999 each, a build time from 1 to 9999 seconds and

## The table

77. The table will have a column for each resource, wood, food, gold and stone, a row for each entry added, and a
    Total row that always shows. [a:1]
78. Each row will have a red × left of the Unit column that removes the row. [a:42]
79. The Unit column's heading will read "Unit" while Buildings is unticked and "Unit/Building" while it is ticked.
    [a:40]
80. # Buildings will take 0 to 99, with a − button left of the number and a + button right of it, both square and as
    tall as the number field. [a:31] [b]
81. A resource column's heading will name the gatherers of its income source, e.g. "# Lumberjacks" for Trees, where
    the name fits, and the plain heading, e.g. "# Wood vils", where it does not. [a:15] [b]
82. Per row and resource, the villagers needed will be the number of buildings × the cost ÷ the time ÷ the gather
    rate. [b]
83. A row's cell will show its villagers to one decimal. [b]
84. Each resource's total will be the exact sum of its rows, rounded up to a whole villager. [a:3] [b]
85. Hovering over a number of villagers will show it to two decimals. [a:4]
86. The Total row will show the total # Buildings in the # Buildings column. [a:32]
87. Villagers required will be shown right of the table, below the technology checkboxes. [a:32]
88. Villagers required will be the totals of every resource whose income source is villagers, plus every builder.
    [a:36] [b]
89. With Generic, Imperial Age, no technology, Farm as the food source, Farm Reseeding and House Building ticked, one
    Knight-line and one Archer will need 5 wood, 6 food and 10 gold villagers, and Villagers required will be 21. [b]
90. A cost or time changed by a bonus or technology will be underlined, and hovering over it will show the base value
    and every effect applied. [b]
91. A row the civ or age cannot make will stay in the table, flagged in red with the reason, e.g. "needs Castle Age".
    [b]
92. A row with 0 buildings will be greyed out. [b]
93. Units will come before buildings in the table, each in the order they were added. [b]
94. Only one entry of a line will be in the table at a time: adding another version will replace the row and keep its
    # Buildings. [a:41] [b]
95. A row of an earlier version of a line will have an Upgrade button, which changes the row to the next version the
    civ can reach. [a:41]
96. Upgrade will be greyed, with the reason, when the civ cannot reach the next version. [a:41] [b]
97. Serjeant (Donjon) will have no Upgrade button. [b]
98. In the wide layout, the table will be at least four rows tall. [b]

## Builders

99. # Builders will show, between Unit and # Buildings, while Buildings is ticked or a building row is in the table.
    [a:36] [b]
100. A building row's # Builders will be 1 by default. [a:36]
101. With n builders, a building will take 3t ÷ (n + 2) seconds of its time t, so a 200-second Castle with 4 builders
     takes 100 seconds. [a:36]
102. A building of 1×1 tiles will take up to 14 builders, 2×2 up to 22, 3×3 up to 33, 4×4 up to 44, 5×5 up to 53, and
     a Gate or Palisade Gate up to 38. [b]
103. Every builder will count toward Villagers required, the default 1 included. [a:36] [b]
104. Farms and Fish Traps will have no # Builders and count no builders. [b]
105. A Farm row will have a box labelled with the Villager's training time, e.g. "25s", which makes the row's time that
     training time. [b]
106. The Stone column will be blank, keeping its space, while Buildings is unticked and no building row is in the
     table. [a:37] [a:38]

## Transition Eco

182. Transition Eco will have the radios "Researching Feudal Age (130s)", "Researching Castle Age (160s)",
     "Researching Imperial Age (190s)" and "After [] seconds". [b]
183. A Researching radio will set the age being left: Researching Feudal Age is Dark Age, Researching Castle Age is
     Feudal Age, and Researching Imperial Age is Castle Age. [b]
184. "After [] seconds" will count as Imperial Age. [b]
185. "After [] seconds" will take whole seconds from 0 to 353,693, with 300 by default. [b]
186. The slider of "After [] seconds" will go from 0 to 900 seconds. [b]
187. Each resource will have a − / + count of gatherers from 0 to 200. [b]
188. A resource's amount will be the gatherers × the gather rate × the time, rounded down, plus what the civ gets on
     reaching the age. [b]
189. With Generic, 10 lumberjacks and Researching Feudal Age, wood will be 504. [b]
190. Research times will include the civ's age-up bonuses, e.g. Malay 78.3 seconds to Feudal Age. [b]
191. Dravidians' 200 wood, and Ethiopians' 100 food and 100 gold, will be added on reaching an age. [b]
192. The Spanish bonus of 20 gold per technology will not count for researching an age. [b]
193. The amounts' heading will read "On reaching <Age>", or with "After [] seconds" the time, e.g. "After 45s",
     "After 5m" or "After 5m30s". [b]
194. Typing an amount will set that resource's count to the fewest gatherers that gather at least that much, up to
     200. [b]
195. Switching to Transition Eco will pick the radio of the age Villagers Required has, and "After [] seconds" for
     Imperial Age. [b]
196. Switching to Transition Eco will set each count to Villagers Required's total for that resource, up to 200. [b]
197. Nothing changed in Transition Eco will change Villagers Required. [b]
198. Clear Values will set every count to 0 and leave the radio and the time of "After [] seconds". [b]
199. Total villagers used will be the sum of the counts of villagers, without Fishing Ships, Feitorias, livestock,
     Trade Carts, Trade Cogs and Whaling Ships. [b]
200. Transition Eco will always show the Stone column. [b]
201. Transition Eco's bonus line will list only the bonuses to gathering and to ages. [b]
202. Relics will not count in Transition Eco. [b]
